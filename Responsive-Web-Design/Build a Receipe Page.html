Nigerian Okro Soup!

Welcome to the ultimate guide for making Nigerian Okro Soup! Follow this simple 4 serving recipe to create delicious, Nigerian Okro Soup that you'll love and enjoy

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds of beef / chicken chuck cut into 1 inch piece
  • 2 teaspoons Kosher Salt divided 1 in the meat water and the other 1 in the soup
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped and divided
  • 4 cups of water depending on the size of the cup but don't let the water to be too much just a little above your meat /chicken
  • 1 pound fresh Okra, tops and buttons trimmed and finely chopped
  • 1/3 cup of red palm oil
  • 1 red bell, 1 habanero pepper (or scotch bonnet) finely and coarsely chopped
  • 2 teaspoons ground dried shrimp (or ground dried cray-fish)
  • 8 ounces smoked mackerel fish skin and bones removed and flaked
  • 4 ounces of fresh spinach (ugu) coarsely chopped

Note: The red bell pepper, habanero pepper depends on how spicy you love your food. If you love a very spicy food use 2-4 red bell and habanero pepper / scotch bonnet and also remember to use fresh pepper.

Instructions

  1. Wash your beef/chicken chuck and put them in a pot of water which the water should be 4 cups / a little above your beef / chicken chucked into pieces
  2. Add your 1 1/2 pounds of beef / chicken chuck into the pot with the water.
  3. Add 2 teaspoons kosher salt, Maggi {knor seasoning cubes}, 1 medium Onion that's finely chopped
  4. Leave the added meat and spice to boil for 10 min
  5. Get your fresh okra; wash it thoroughly and trim the tops and bottom and finely chop it.
  6. After the 10-15 min add 1/3 cup of red palm oil into the meat water.
  7. Add the chopped red bell, habanero pepper or scotch bonnet with your fresh red pepper.
  8. Add 2 tablespoons of grounded dried shrimps or cray-fish into the water on fire.
  9. Add 8 ounces of smoked mackerel skin {dry fish, / black-fish, / smoked-fish} which the bones has been removed and flaked.
  10. Leave it for 30-45 min for all the added ingredients to boil and cover your pot.
  11. After boiling add your 4 ounces of washed fresh spinach {ugu} which is coarsely chopped / cutted leave it for 5-10 min to steam with the soup and stir it after 10 min you can serve it with {Semovita / Semolina / Eba(garri)}.
